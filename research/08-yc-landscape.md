# YC Competitive Landscape for Lethe

## Method note

The YC directory is JS-driven, but YC exposes an **undocumented public JSON API** — `https://api.ycombinator.com/v0.1/companies?batch=W25&page=N` — which returns `name, slug, website, oneLiner, longDescription, teamSize, batch, tags, industries, status (Active/Inactive/Acquired), url`. I pulled **every company in W23, S23, W24, S24, F24, W25, X25(=P25), S25, F25, W26, X26(=P26), S26, F26 — 2,398 companies** — and keyword-filtered locally, then verified with HN's Algolia API and direct site fetches. This is an exhaustive enumeration, not a sample.

⚠️ **Funding caveat:** the session's WebSearch budget was exhausted early (200/200) and search engines block WebFetch, so funding is only reported where I found a primary source. Most YC seed companies here have **no disclosed round** — assume YC standard deal (~$500K) unless stated. Every unsourced number is flagged.

## What this implies for the YC application

**The good news: YC has never funded Lethe's actual primitive.** Across 2,398 companies in 13 batches, nobody is doing *reversible* identity tokenization with *local key custody* and *per-provider unlinkable surrogates*. LogosGuard (F25) is one-way placeholder redaction in a browser extension. Credal did reversible-ish redaction in W23, raised $4.8M from Spark, and then repositioned away from it. Tinfoil solves the same user problem via enclaves but **explicitly cannot serve GPT-5 or Claude** — Lethe's whole premise, non-profiling access to *frontier* models, is the gap Tinfoil's architecture structurally cannot close. That contrast is the single strongest sentence available for the application.

**The bad news: half of Lethe is crowded, and it's the half that's easiest to describe.** "Agent gateway with per-agent data scopes and an audit ledger" now has at least six active YC bets in four consecutive batches — Alter (S25), Multifactor (F25), Agentic Fabriq (W26), Decawork (S26), Clawvisor (P26), plus Golf, Salus, Capacitive, Metorial and Executor adjacent. Leading with the agent gateway will read as me-too to any partner who has seen the last three batches. Likewise, the gateway/router layer is fully commoditized (LiteLLM at 59k stars, Experiential Labs, Respan, Conifer, dari.dev, Speko, Amorphic, Corsair, Carrot Labs) and Helicone — the best-known YC gateway — just got absorbed into Mintlify into maintenance mode. **Lead with the privacy primitive; treat the gateway as the delivery mechanism, not the product.**

**Prepare for one specific objection.** Tinfoil's own YC profile dismisses this category in writing: PII redaction is a *"band-aid that doesn't work in practice"*, and the HN commentary on their launch agreed — redaction either destroys the context that makes the model useful, or rests on unverifiable promises. That is the exact objection a partner will put to Lethe in the interview. Reversibility plus local key custody plus per-provider unlinkability *is* the technical answer (context is preserved because surrogates are consistent and restorable; trust isn't required because the key never leaves the user), but it has to be the first thing said, not the fourth.

**Also prepare for the graveyard question.** Blyss (W23) had a Stanford/Apple/NVIDIA crypto team, the correct thesis, and died; Tesseral died a year after raising $3.3M; Nuntius died after one batch; Galini, Unbound, Credal and PromptArmor all walked away from this exact wedge. The honest, strong framing is that every one of those was a **2023–2024 bet made before agents created a forcing function** — Blyss was selling privacy to humans who didn't yet feel the loss; Lethe is selling it into a world where agents are about to transact on users' behalf at scale. The counter-evidence that the category is real and now: Infisical went YC → $2.8M → **$16M Series A led by Elad Gil**, and Flower raised **$20M Series A**.

**Finally, the consumer lane is nearly empty, and one company just took it.** Egoist Machines (S26, YC partner Harshita Arora) is building "AI Passport" — portable personal context, permissioned per-app access with purpose and duration, revocation, audit receipts, and a local-hardware roadmap. That is Lethe's consent-and-ledger architecture aimed at memory portability rather than unlinkability. They are the company to name explicitly in the competitors box and to differentiate against cleanly: Egoist makes your context *portable*; Lethe makes your identity *unlinkable*. YC clearly likes the underlying thesis — it also funded SpruceID (W21) on *"platforms should sign into people's data vaults."*
