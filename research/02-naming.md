## 1. LETHE deep dive

**The sources.** Lethe is first a *daimona*: in Hesiod's *Theogony* 226–227 she is a child of Eris (Strife), sister to Ponos, Limos, Ate and Horkos (Oath) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethe_(daughter_of_Eris)), [Theoi](https://www.theoi.com/Daimon/Lethe.html)). Plato's Myth of Er (*Republic* 10.621a) makes her a place: souls cross the "plain of Lethe" to the *Ameles potamos*, the River of Unmindfulness, "whose water no vessel can hold"; all must drink a measure, and those not saved by wisdom drink more than the measure ([Myth of Er](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myth_of_Er), [Lethe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethe)). Virgil, *Aeneid* 6.703–751: souls destined for new bodies drink "carefree draughts and long oblivion" before returning ([Theoi Aeneid 6](https://www.theoi.com/Text/VirgilAeneid6.html)).

**The Orphic material is where it gets useful.** The Petelia gold tablet (4th–3rd c. BC) tells the initiate: there is a spring on the left by a white cypress — *do not approach it*; further on is cold water flowing from the Lake of Memory (Mnemosyne), with guardians; declare "I am a child of Earth and starry Heaven" and they will let you drink ([Petelia Gold Tablet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petelia_Gold_Tablet)). Pausanias 9.39 describes the same pairing as a live ritual at the oracle of Trophonius: the consultant drinks Lethe to forget, then Mnemosyne to remember what he sees below ([Pausanias 9.39](https://periegesis.abm.uu.se/en/reports.php?reportid=317)). Dante adds a third river: after Lethe erases the memory of sin, *Eunoe* restores the memory of good (*Purg.* 33).

**Aletheia.** *Lēthē* comes from *lanthanō*, "escape notice, be hidden" (middle: "forget"). *Alētheia* = *a-* + *lēthē*: un-hiddenness, un-forgetting. Heidegger built his theory of truth-as-unconcealment on this ([ontology.co](https://www.ontology.co/heidegger-aletheia.htm)). Related: *lethargy* is literally *lēthē + argos*; *lethal* is from Latin *letum* but its spelling was altered by association with *lēthēs hydōr* ([Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/lethal)). Both negative neighbours are real.

**Existing uses.** Heavy in exactly this neighbourhood: PyPI `lethe-ml` (machine unlearning) and `lethe` (git tool); arXiv *LetheViT* (unlearning); ACM "Lethe: Secure Deletion by Addition"; GitHub drive-wipers, a Tor/I2P self-destructing messenger, a video anonymizer, and an AI-agent *memory* layer ([openlethe/lethe](https://github.com/openlethe/lethe)). Consumer: Lethe Press, *Lethe – Episode One* (2016 game), a Dark Tranquillity song, a Fire Emblem character, a personal AI assistant at app.lethe.gg, a Polish apparel company. USPTO: LETHE Reg. 6828918 (wines/spirits, 2022); no Class 9/42 mark confirmed — run TESS/EUIPO before filing. Domains: lethe.ai (Vite boilerplate), lethe.com (bare login), getlethe.com ("Lethe") all held; lethe.app and lethe.dev returned no DNS (probably unregistered; verify).

**Verdict.** *Strengths:* two syllables, an unmatched literary spine, and the Lethe/Mnemosyne dichotomy hands you a whole product vocabulary for free. *Weaknesses:* (1) pronunciation split ("LEE-thee" vs "leth"); (2) death/amnesia halo; (3) most seriously, in ML circles *Lethe* now signals **machine unlearning** — post-hoc erasure, which is the promise this product explicitly refuses to rely on. The product is *never knew*, not *forgot*. *Framing that fixes it:* "Oblivion by construction. The model drinks from Lethe; you keep Mnemosyne." Or: "Nothing to forget, because nothing arrived."

## 3. Ranking

1. **LETHE** — best sound and depth; risks addressable with framing and an early Class 9/42 filing.
2. **EIDOLON** — truest metaphor for reversible tokenization; a real English word in the Anthropic register.
3. **KYNEE** — most ownable; reads like infrastructure (cf. Kyber).
4. **AMELES** — Plato's own name for the river; "aimless" hazard.
5. **NEPHELE** — strongest concept-plus-pun; premium domain.

**Recommendation:** Primary **LETHE** ("oblivion by construction"); fallback **EIDOLON**; clean-room option **KYNEE**. Domains: lethe.app / lethe.dev (appear open), uselethe.com, lethe.sh.
