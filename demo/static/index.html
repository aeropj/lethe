Without Lethe, the lab also gets
  • your account
  • your card
  • your IP
  • your real words
  • every past chat, linked
With Lethe, it gets
  • one valid request from Lethe's pool
  • stand-ins instead of names
  • no account, card or IP of yours
  • nothing linking this chat to your others

In this local POC the gateway runs on your machine, so OpenAI sees your API key and your IP. In production the relay and gateway are separate operators and the key is Lethe's.

Demo-grade cryptography, not audited: RSA-FDH blind signatures (the idea behind Privacy Pass), X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305 sealing in the shape of Oblivious HTTP, padding to fixed sizes. All five parties run in one local process so you can inspect them.
